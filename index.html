Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 Version 1.0.0

Image Edit does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data.

This is not a policy commitment that someone has to remember to keep. The app does not request permission to use the internet, so Android will not let it open a network connection at all. It cannot send your photos anywhere, and it works normally in airplane mode.

What the app does with your images

Every operation runs on your device using your device's own processor: editing, filters, retouching, scanning, text recognition and PDF creation. Nothing is sent anywhere for processing.

Your original files are never modified. When you save a project, the app makes its own private copy to work from and leaves your file untouched.

What is stored, and where

The following is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read:

  • Projects you save, including a private copy of the source image
  • Signatures and custom stickers you create
  • Export presets you save
  • Thumbnails of recently opened images
  • Your settings, such as theme and export defaults

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. You can clear temporary files at any time in Settings → Storage inside the app.

Images you export are saved where you choose: your gallery, a folder you pick, or an app you share to. Once exported, they are handled by Android and by whichever app you sent them to.

Permissions

Permission Why When it is asked for
CAMERA To photograph documents in the scanner Only when you open the scanner
VIBRATE Haptic feedback on sliders and controls Never prompted
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE To save an exported image to your gallery on Android 9 and older Only on those older versions

The app deliberately does not request access to your photo library. It uses Android's own photo picker instead, which shows the app only the specific images you select.

Check it yourself

You do not have to take any of this on trust. On your phone:

Settings → Apps → Image Edit → Permissions

There is no network or internet entry, because the app never asked for one.

Text recognition

The Extract text feature uses Google's ML Kit text recognition with the model bundled inside the app. Recognition happens on your device. No image or recognised text is sent to Google or to anyone else. Because the app has no internet permission, no such transmission is possible.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics library, no advertising library, no crash reporting service and no tracking identifiers. There is no account and no sign-in.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children, and has no social or content-sharing features beyond Android's own share sheet.
